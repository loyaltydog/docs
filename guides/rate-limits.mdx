title: "Rate limits"
description: "Per-merchant monthly allowance and minute and hour throttles for hashed public keys."
keywords: ["rate limit", "429", "quota"]
---

The allowance and throttles for hashed public keys (`ld_*`) are **per merchant account**. They are shared across all of a merchant's keys. They are not per key. They are not per IP. Creating more keys does not add allowance. JWT App Keys are **not** in this limiter (SWE-1181).

| Plan | Monthly calls | Per hour | Per minute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 100,000 | 1,000 | 60 |
| Growth | 250,000 | 2,000 | 120 |
| Scale | 600,000 | 5,000 | 300 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

Hitting the per-minute or per-hour limit returns **429** with code `rate_limited` and a `Retry-After` header. Headers on `ld_*` responses are `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining`, and `X-RateLimit-Reset`. `Retry-After` is sent on 429 only. `Remaining` is `0` on every 429. The `X-RateLimit-*` headers on a 429 describe the limit that was hit.

Hitting the monthly allowance returns **429** with code `monthly_quota_exceeded`. It is a hard stop until the allowance resets on the 1st of the month (UTC).

You get an email, and the dashboard shows a banner, at 80% and 100% of the monthly allowance. There are no overage charges.

Cloudflare also applies an edge ceiling on `api.loyalty.dog` (300 requests / 10s / IP) for flood protection. Dashboard and POS browser origins skip that ceiling. Server-to-server JWT traffic without an `Origin` header counts toward it.

## Watching your usage without writing code

The **API Keys** page in your [dashboard](https://app.loyalty.dog/account/appkeys) shows a live quota card for each hashed key: requests used this month, error and average-latency totals for the last 30 days, and a CSV export of the underlying time series. Usage counts toward the merchant's shared monthly allowance. A banner appears at 80% and 100% of that allowance, and you'll get an email at each of those thresholds too. This view is dashboard-only — it isn't part of the public API.
